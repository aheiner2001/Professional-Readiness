# Interview Prep: Questions, Answers, and Suggestions

## 1) Tell me something about yourself.
- I’m a student who has spent the last several years balancing school and hands-on work.
- I’ve built projects across Python, machine learning, databases, and AI-focused tools.
- I’m motivated by solving practical problems and continuously improving how work gets done.

## 2) What are some of your strengths?
- Quick learner who adapts fast to new tools and workflows.
- Hard-working, detail-oriented, and accountable.
- Process improver: in window washing, I tested better techniques/equipment and helped improve team practices.
- Strong blend of technical range and practical execution.

## 3) Why should we hire you?
- I bring broad technical experience across software and AI-related projects.
- I build with long-term scalability in mind, even in early-stage work.
- I contribute quickly, take feedback well, and improve fast.
- I’m eager to help the team succeed while continuing to grow my skills.

## 4) What are some of your weaknesses?
- I used to try to build everything at once instead of delivering in smaller steps.
- I’ve improved by breaking work into focused pieces, shipping incrementally, and getting feedback early.
- This has made me more organized, efficient, and collaborative.

## 5) Why do you want to work for us?
- I connect with your mission and the impact your work has on people.
- I value meaningful work that people genuinely appreciate.
- I want to contribute to a team with strong culture while continuing to develop professionally.

## 6) What do you know about our company?
- I’ve researched your company and reviewed your mission and public content.
- Your work appears purpose-driven, and that aligns with what motivates me.
- I’m especially interested in how your organization creates value for the people you serve.

## 7) How would your previous employer describe you?
- Hard-working and dependable.
- Detail-focused and efficient.
- Independent, but collaborative when needed.
- Accountable for outcomes and committed to high-quality results.

## 8) Where do you see yourself in five years?
- Growing into a strong technical contributor in AI/software.
- Taking on larger ownership and helping drive impactful projects.
- Working in a healthy, high-performance culture where I can create real value.

## 9) What do you expect as a salary or compensation?
- I’m open to discussion based on scope, team fit, and total learning opportunity.
- From my research, comparable internship ranges vary, and I’m flexible within market-appropriate bands.
- A practical target range for me is around **$20–$25/hour**, with openness to adjust based on role expectations.

## 10) Do you have any questions for me?
- What does day-to-day work look like for this role?
- What would success look like in the first 30, 60, and 90 days?
- What were previous interns’ typical daily tasks and biggest contributions?
- What are your top expectations for someone in this position?
- How does the team give feedback and support growth?

## Separate Section: Suggestions to Improve Each Answer

- **Tell me about yourself:** Keep it to 60–90 seconds and use present → past → future structure.
- **Strengths:** Pick 2–3 strengths and attach one quick proof/example to each.
- **Why hire you:** Connect your skills directly to what this specific role needs most.
- **Weaknesses:** Name a real weakness, then emphasize the system you use to improve it.
- **Why us:** Mention mission + culture + what you specifically want to contribute.
- **What do you know about us:** Reference specific facts from their website, products, or recent updates.
- **Previous employer description:** Use language from real performance feedback if possible.
- **Five years:** Show ambition plus flexibility; focus on growth and contribution.
- **Compensation:** Stay confident and collaborative; anchor to research and role scope.
- **Questions for interviewer:** Ask about success metrics, mentorship, team process, and growth path.

## Extra Questions You Can Ask at the End

- What are the biggest priorities for this role in the next few months?
- What qualities made your best intern(s) successful here?
- What challenges is the team currently trying to solve?
- How do you evaluate intern performance?
- What does onboarding and training look like?
- What is the timeline for next steps?
